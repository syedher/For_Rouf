favourite person ♡

There are things I feel every day that I don't always know how to put into words. So, today, I wrote them down for you.

To my Baby

A little letter, just for you

To my favourite human,

Hey, jaanu...

I honestly don't know where to begin. There is so much I want to tell you, so many little feelings I've been carrying around, and somehow even a thousand words don't feel enough when it comes to you.

First of all, I hope you know how much you mean to me. Not just on special days, not just when everything is going perfectly, but on the ordinary days too. On the days when we're busy, when we're tired, when we don't have anything extraordinary to talk about. When we are fighting and just want to leave. When leaving feels like the only option.Even then, you're one of my favourite parts of being alive.

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes for just a moment. Maybe then you'd understand why I'm so proud of you. I see how hard you try. I see the effort you put into your responsibilities, the dreams you carry, and the pressure you place on yourself. I know there are moments when things feel uncertain and when the future seems like a lot to figure out.

And I know I may not always understand every struggle you're going through. I might not have the perfect words or know exactly how to make things better. But I want you to know that I see you trying. I notice the little things. I notice your strength, even when you don't feel particularly strong.

You're a good son, Rofi. You're someone who cares, someone who wants to build a good life, someone who keeps going even when the road isn't as easy as you'd hoped. I hope you give yourself credit for that. You don't have to achieve everything at once to be worthy of love, respect, or happiness.

I believe in your dreams. I believe in the person you're becoming and in all the beautiful things you're working towards. There will be difficult days, disappointments, delays, and moments when you question yourself. When those days come, I hope you remember that one difficult chapter doesn't define your entire story. And that I'll always stay with you. That I'll choose you no matter what.

And when your hard work finally turns into the life you've imagined, I hope you pause for a moment and feel proud of the person who made it there. I hope you remember all the days you kept going, even when nobody could see how much effort it took. I'll be cheering for you through every little victory along the way.

I also want you to know that you don't always have to be strong around me. You can have bad days. You can feel lost, frustrated, quiet, or tired. You don't need to pretend that everything is fine just to make me happy. I want the real you, in all your moods and moments. I want to be someone you can come to, not another thing you have to be perfect for. I'll not make you feel weak. I'll not think you are weak just coz you cried infront of me. I'll never.

And about us... I hope we keep choosing each other through all the versions of life waiting for us. I hope we keep laughing at silly things, sharing our little stories, making memories, learning from our mistakes, and growing together. I don't expect everything to be perfect. I just want us to keep being honest, patient, and kind to each other.

If there's one thing I want you to never doubt, it's that my love isn't dependent on how successful you are, how productive your day was, or how close you are to achieving your goals. I love you for being you. Your dreams matter to me, but so does your peace. Your future matters to me, but so does the person you are right now.

I can't promise that every day will be easy. But I can promise that I want to keep showing up, keep understanding you, keep making space for your feelings, and keep reminding you of how loved you are.

So, Baba, take your time. Chase your dreams. Rest when you need to. Be proud of the little progress. And whenever the world feels a little too heavy, remember that you have someone who believes in you with her whole heart.

I am so, so proud of you. Not only for what you might become someday, but for who you are today. And if I could choose all over again, I'd still want to find my way to you.

With all my love, Your girl, always ♡
