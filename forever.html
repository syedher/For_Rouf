A little piece of my heart

My dearest Bacha,

If I could give you one thing in this entire world, it would be the ability to see yourself through my eyes. Maybe then you'd understand why I adore you so much.

I don't think love is always about grand gestures, perfect days, or having everything figured out. Sometimes, it's just finding someone whose presence makes the world feel a little softer. Someone who makes the simplest moments worth remembering. For me, that's you.

I love the little things about you. The way you talk, the way you laugh, the way you get excited about things, and even the little habits that you probably don't notice yourself. I love the person you are when nobody is watching, too.

I know life won't always be easy. There will be days when we're tired, when things don't go as planned, and when we might not have all the right words. But I hope we keep choosing to understand each other, to listen, to grow, and to make room for each other's dreams.

I want to celebrate every little victory of yours. I want to be there when you achieve the things you've worked so hard for. And when things get difficult, I hope I can be a safe place for you to rest for a while.

Thank you for being part of my life, for giving me memories I can revisit with a smile, and for making my days feel a little more beautiful just by being in them. I may not always express it perfectly, but I hope you never question how much you mean to me.

And if we could go back to the beginning and do it all over again, I'd still want to meet you. I'd still want to know you, laugh with you, make memories with you, and fall for you in all the little ways that make our story ours.

So here's my little promise: I'll keep finding reasons to love you, even in the ordinary days. I'll keep cheering for you, reminding you of your worth, and making memories with you wherever life takes us.

This website may have a last page, but my affection for you doesn't end here. There are still so many conversations to have, places to see, silly moments to share, and beautiful memories waiting for us.

I love you, Rofi. More than this little page could ever hold.

Always yours,
